How it works

Three agents feed one triage card, routed by a supervisor

Agent 2
Evidence

Cause frequencies at 4 levels (catalog + symptom → family → all), parts and areas per cause, serial history.

Agent 3
Score + output

triage_card() plus what to do from the troubleshooting manual, family risk scorecards and dashboards. Scored on a 2026 holdout.

4

Recommendations · Agent 3

Each likely cause comes with what to do next

R1394 unit · #1 Component Failure · PCBA power board

Manual §1: PCBA / Circuitry, Component Failure

  1. Power down and discharge per lockout/tagout before opening.
  2. Inspect the PCBA for scorching, cracked packages, bulging capacitors, cold joints.
  3. Check primary power rails for shorts before re-energizing.
  4. Confirm a suspect component with an in- or out-of-circuit test.
  5. Replace at board level by reference designator, not the whole assembly.
  6. Re-run the full functional test before return to service.
